MVP prototype · CS-2430 Technological Entrepreneurship

Every parcel.
One secure pickup.

ParcelPoint replaces the "left at the entrance" dorm delivery loop with one secure hub, one courier check-in and one one-time QR pickup — with a timestamped record of every hand-off. This clickable prototype demonstrates the full student and courier journey of the MVP.

Demo runs in your browser (no backend). Seeded student: room 214, phone last 4 digits 0417.

How it works

1

Drop

Courier checks in every parcel for the building at one hub in a single stop.

2

Notify

Student receives a Telegram / SMS message with a one-time QR code.

3

Pick up

Student scans at the locker any time; the hand-off is logged automatically.

MVP boundary: this prototype proves the check-in → notification → one-time QR → audit loop. Locker hardware, carrier API integration, payments and multi-campus rollout are deliberately outside the MVP. Data lives in your browser and can be restored with Reset demo.
